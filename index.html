01 / ABOUT

Curiosity is the
starting point.

I study physics at the Universidad Mayor de San Andrés (UMSA) in La Paz, Bolivia. My interests lie in observational astronomy, stellar variability, astrophysics and the development of scientific instrumentation.

My observational work includes photometric studies of pulsating variable stars, including KZ Hydrae and TT Lyncis. I also participate in technical projects involving Cherenkov detectors, astronomical outreach and the analysis of scientific data.
