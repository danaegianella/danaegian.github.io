02 About

I study physics at the Universidad Mayor de San Andrés and work within the O.R.B.I.T.A. program at the Cota Cota Astronomical Observatory. My main research interest is the detection and characterization of exoplanets through high-precision photometry. I built my observational foundation on pulsating variable stars, and I have also worked with the TTT-80 robotic telescope at Teide Observatory. 03 Research

Research direction: exoplanet transit photometry

Main line of interest · building on my work in time-series photometry
  • Detecting and characterizing transiting planets: differential photometry, systematics control, detrending and light-curve fitting.
  • Treating stellar variability and pulsation as a noise source to be modelled, a direct link with my work on δ Scuti and SX Phoenicis stars.
  • Secondary interest: photometric variability of brown dwarfs.

Foundation: photometric campaign on KZ Hydrae and TT Lyncis

O.R.B.I.T.A. · Cota Cota Observatory, UMSA · Tutor: Dr. Roy Bustos · year – present
  • Multi-session observations (Dec 2025 – Feb 2026) of an SX Phoenicis star (KZ Hya) and a δ Scuti star (TT Lyn) in Sloan g′, r′ and i′.
  • Pipeline: differential aperture photometry (AstroImageJ), Fourier and cubic-spline fits, Lomb–Scargle periodograms, and comparison with published periods.
  • Anomalous gap/jump in the KZ Hya light curve of 26 Dec 2025, supporting a binarity hypothesis (cf. Fu et al. 2008).
  • Custom Python scripts for post-processing; technical report written in LaTeX.
